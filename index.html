Hi, I’m Federica Ballardini.

I'm a geographer & a cartographer.
I craft research and maps that blend people, landscapes, and stories.

Welcome to my digital portfolio!

I’m Federica, but everyone calls me Fede. I was born near the Dolomites, in Northern Italy, but since then I’ve lived in many places, including Berlin, the South of France, Taipei, Munich, Vienna and Dresden. Travelling and exploration have always been my passions, and through cartography, I found a way to translate the beauty and diversity of the world into maps and graphics.

Even though cartography is my specialisation, I am also proficient in using GIS for spatial analysis and I have a solid background in Remote Sensing, Environmental Sciences and Geography.

Before moving into cartography, I worked in field ethnography, grant writing and NGO management in the context of migration. Today, I can often be found at a desk with QGIS, Colab, Adobe Illustrator, Visual Studio Code and a couple of Google documents open at the same time.

The rest of my time, I spend running on trails, climbing up walls and walking ungodly distances.
